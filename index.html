About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Political Science at Georgia State University, specializing in Comparative Politics with a focus on legislative politics across Africa. My current research examines how African legislatures can effectively exercise their institutional oversight to curb executive corruption. Broadly, my work explores executive-legislative relations, political parties, democratic consolidation, and electoral dynamics in emerging democracies.
